About MELLEMO

MELLEMO started with a school project and a simple question: What if you could turn a tabletop adventure into something you could put on your wall? We create interactive, choose-your-own-adventure posters inspired by the world of TTRPGs. Each poster invites you to make choices, follow the story, and see where your adventure takes you — whether you’re completely new to tabletop roleplaying or have been rolling dice for years. Our goal is to make TTRPGs feel a little more approachable, a little more playful, and easy to experience in a new way. You don’t need to know every rule or have a whole adventuring party ready to go. You just need to be curious and willing to see what happens next. What started as a school project quickly became something we genuinely believe in. We love the creativity, storytelling, and community that come with roleplaying, and we want to create things that help more people discover that feeling for themselves.

So grab your dice, point to a path, and choose your adventure.

About Our Mission

At MELLEMO, we believe stories are better when you get to be part of them. Our mission is to create playful, human-made experiences that bring people closer to storytelling, TTRPGs, and each other. We want to make roleplaying approachable for newcomers while giving experienced adventurers something fun and unexpected to explore. We believe roleplaying can do more than tell stories. It can foster community, help combat loneliness and bullying, and create safe spaces where people feel welcome and free to be themselves. Through shared adventures and creative play, we hope to help people connect, build friendships, and learn from one another. Everything we create is rooted in our love for human-made stories, creativity, and craftsmanship. We want our products to feel personal and tangible in an increasingly digital world.

Our mission is simple: make stories interactive, adventures approachable, and create opportunities for people to play, connect, and belong. .
